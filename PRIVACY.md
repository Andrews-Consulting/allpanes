# allPanes privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

allPanes is a Chrome extension made by Andrews Consulting. It takes screenshots of web pages,
including the content of every area on the page that scrolls on its own. This policy explains what
it does with your information. In short: everything stays on your computer.

## What allPanes stores

Everything allPanes keeps is stored in your browser, on your computer:

- **Your two settings** from the Options page: the default download format (PNG or JPEG) and the
  JPEG quality. They are kept in this browser only and are not synced to your other devices.
- **Your 10 most recent captures**, so a capture's tab can be reopened or reloaded. Each one holds
  the screenshot, the title and address of the page it was taken from, when it was taken, and
  technical details such as the image's size. When you take an 11th capture, the oldest is deleted
  automatically.

## What allPanes reads from a page

allPanes does nothing until you click its toolbar button or press its keyboard shortcut, and then
works only on the tab you're looking at. To take the screenshot, it measures the page's scrolling
areas, temporarily makes them tall enough to show all their content, takes the screenshot, and puts
the page back as it was. Besides the screenshot itself, it reads only the page's title and address,
which it uses to label the capture and name the file.

It does not read or keep the page's text, anything you've typed into forms, passwords, cookies, or
your browsing history.

## What allPanes sends

Nothing. allPanes makes no network requests. It has no analytics, no advertising, no account or
sign-up, and uses no third-party services. Your captures and settings are never uploaded, sold or
shared with anyone.

When you click **Download**, the image is saved to your computer like any other download. When you
click **Copy image**, it's placed on your clipboard. From then on the file or image is yours to do
with as you choose; allPanes doesn't send it anywhere.

## Permissions

- **debugger**: used only while taking a capture, and only on the tab being captured, to resize
  that tab's view and take the screenshot. Chrome shows a "started debugging this browser" notice
  while it's in use. allPanes detaches as soon as the capture is done.
- **storage**: used only to save your two settings, in this browser.

## Deleting your data

Removing allPanes from Chrome deletes its settings and its stored captures. Images you've
downloaded stay in your Downloads folder until you delete them yourself.
